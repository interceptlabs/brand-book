Logo and lockup.

Clear-space & sizing

  • Clear-space equals one triangle height on every side.
  • Minimum size is 80px wide on screen, 20mm wide in print.
  • Aspect stays at 4.25 : 1. Never stretch, squish, or rotate.
  • Pair the neutral — Carbon wordmark on light, Halo wordmark on dark.

Don't

  • Recolor outside Flarepop, Coolsweep, or Wiretree.
  • Overlap headlines, body, CTAs, or functional UI.
  • Recreate the wordmark in CSS or set it in ALL CAPS.
  • Add drop-shadows, gradients, blurs, or scrim effects.
